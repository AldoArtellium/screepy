# Decisions: defense

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

## Defense (`src/defense/`, Hivemind `room-defense.ts`, `manager.military.ts`, `process/rooms/owned/defense.ts`)

- `enemyStrength(room)`: NONE / WEAK (invaders only, or towers out-heal and walls hold) / NORMAL / STRONG / DEADLY, from the hostiles' attack and heal (boosts included), the towers, the energy capacity and the weakest planned rampart. `isWallIntact`: every planned main rampart built. `desiredWallStrength`: the threat-scaled target below (x2 during the safe mode cooldown); builders repair ramparts up to it (on-ramps to a tenth).
- Rampart hits scaled to the threat (`defense/RoomDefense.ts` `wallTarget`, `updateThreat`; The International `commune.ts` minRampartHits, `defenceProcs.ts` manageThreat; Hivemind: maxWallHealth[rcl]). Target ((rcl-3) x 50)^2.5 + threat x 5 x rcl^2, at least 20k, at most maxWallHealth: 20k at RCL 3-4, 100k at 5, 276k at 6, 566k at 7, 988k at 8 without threat. The safe-mode multipliers are kept and on-ramps still get a tenth. `room.memory.threat` rises to the strength of a player attack that is NORMAL or worse (dismantle + melee x 1.2 + ranged x 3.5 + heal; invaders never count) and otherwise decays x0.9999 per tick. It is never below 0.1 x the highest `Memory.military.infamy`. The `checkWallIntegrity` endgame gate (12.5k/50k/75k/500k) stays below the target. Ramparts are only placed once a storage exists (TI `needsStoringStructure`; Hivemind: RCL 3). New rooms start with a 20000-tick safe mode during which no rampart is built, so 20k from-scratch runs barely change. Mockup from baseline states, 12000 ticks, single runs, points gained: W1N3 (state at 18k, no storage) 47.4k -> 53.1k with the target alone, 58.0k with the storage wait too. W9N5: target alone 128.5k, with the wait 127.4k. W7N3 (state at 22k, RCL 4 -> 5) 564k -> 610k with raids (outcome identical: cleared in 34/37 ticks). W7N3 forced to RCL 5: 642k -> 704k, `work:repair` 5928 -> 1871, rampart average 186k -> 74k. Not tested against a player attack (the mockup has none).
- Towers: shoot the hostile with the highest military priority (our damage minus its healing, times its value), only if it can't just drain them at the room edge; else heal wounded fighters (any wounded creep without enemies); else, from NORMAL enemies with half a tower of energy, repair the weakest rampart a hostile can hit. Towers repair nothing else.
- Harmless hostiles (`RoomDefense.ts` `harmlessTarget`, not in Hivemind, which only shoots `isDangerous` creeps): after the attack and heal steps, each tower with half its energy kills a MOVE / TOUGH-only creep it one-shots this tick (healers around counted), closest first, one target per tower (counter `tower:attackHarmless`). shard0 W28N7 (2026-10-24, doorknobnob from W28N8, ticks ~78527500-78536500): up to 27 single-MOVE `blocker` creeps parked on the only passages to the controller (x=14, y 1-6), to a source pocket (32-34,17) and between the base and the north exit (19,7)-(27,14), never shot; transporters and builders piled up against them, the towers ran dry (90 then 0 energy for 1000+ ticks: dismantlers never shot) and the spawn starved (a 340-energy guardian). 1-WORK `decoy` creeps at the edge and `drainer`s drained the towers first. Mockup `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3 --blockers W7N3:8:500`, 2500 ticks: before 8 blockers parked all along (16 spawned, renewed at 1500 ticks), storage 34.6k, progress 9713; after 46 spawned, 45 harmless shots, none parked, storage 43.0k, progress 10166-10195. Home raids (`--raid 1500:smallMelee+smallRanged,2500:bigMelee+bigRanged`) unchanged: cleared after 14 / 68 ticks.
- Threat score (`defense/threat.ts`, pure model `defense/threatModel.ts`, offline check `node tests/threat/run.mjs`, in CI; not in Hivemind, which values a creep by its parts: HEAL 5, CLAIM 10, RANGED 2, ATTACK / WORK 1): per hostile and tick, attack power (`creepPower`: ATTACK, RANGED_ATTACK, CLAIM, WORK as dismantle, boosts included; destroyed parts count 10%, Hivemind's floor: at 0 a crippled invader with only MOVE left was never shot again and raids lived 1500 ticks) x2 when in reach of one of our structures (1, 3 for ranged) x1.5 within 3 of a spawn, the storage, the terminal, a tower or the controller; a healer adds its heal + half the threat of the most threatening hostile within 3. Used by the tower priority (instead of Hivemind's militaryValue), the guardians and the squads. Mockup `--preset plan5 --enemy W8N3:5 --siege W7N3:1500`, 3500 ticks (towers killed medics first, value 20 vs a dismantler's 8): hits dismantled on the target rampart 42750 -> 0 (the towers now strip the working dismantler's front WORK parts: its dismantles fail with ERR_NO_BODYPART), lowest tower energy 930 -> 1480, tower shots 174 -> 302 (decoys on a rampart are shot too), 22 dismantlers killed both. Home raids (`--preset rcl4 --raid 1500:smallMelee+smallRanged,2500:bigMelee+bigRanged`): cleared after 14 / 68 -> 14 / 58 ticks; boosted remote raid (`1500:smallMelee*@W7N4`): 208 ticks both. Safe mode left as in Hivemind (it already waits for an incomplete wall, nothing to measure).
- Guardians (`roles/guardian.ts`) hold the free main rampart closest to the hostile with the best (distance to the guardian / 2 + distance to the hostile + 1) / threat; Hivemind weighs every dangerous hostile alike. W28N7: 1-WORK decoys at (27,11-13) drew the guardian to (26,12) while two 8-WORK dismantlers broke the rampart (22,10). In the siege the towers already stop the dismantlers: the guardian's own gain is not measured.
- Idle cost (no hostile in the room): `enemyStrength` returns NONE before counting towers or scanning ramparts, and the towers are only looked up when a creep of ours is wounded. Active tower ids are cached per room (`activeTowers`: `isActive()` walks every structure) until the RCL or the tower count changes, else ~1000 ticks +-10%; the planned main rampart tiles are cached per plan object (`lowestWallStrength`). Under attack the logic is unchanged. Profile (mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl5`, 3000 ticks, idle): `enemyStrength` 0.21 CPU/tick (0.17 of it `lowestWallStrength`: `plan.getLocations` + a full structure find), the tower find with `isActive` 0.14, done twice. Module 0.46 -> 0.13 CPU/tick (`--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3`, 2000 ticks: 0.26 -> 0.13); raids at 1500 / 2500 (`smallMelee+smallRanged`, `bigMelee+bigRanged`): first tower shot on ticks 1500 and 2500 both, cleared after 8 / 34 ticks, 84 tower attacks (baseline: 8 / 30-34, 76-84).
- Safe mode: enemies present (any strength with a single room), ramparts not complete, a spawn exists and a dangerous hostile is within 2 (4 if ranged) of a spawn, the storage, the controller...
- Spawn: remote defenders (spawn role `brawler`, see remote mining) and owned room defense (`roomDefense`): brawler below RCL 4, also during a safe mode (deviation: Hivemind waits for its last 500 ticks, so an early invader lived through the first room's 20000-tick safe mode); from RCL 4 and NORMAL enemies, up to 5 guardians (1 ranged per 4) at priority 5 and up to 5 emergency builders (priority 4) with 10000 energy. Remote spawning stops while enemies at home are NORMAL or stronger.
- Boosts (Hivemind room-defense getCreepBoosts): guardians ATTACK or RANGED_ATTACK, emergency builders WORK (repair), best compound in stock, tier at most the enemies' (no limit up to NORMAL enemies, as in Hivemind). See Labs and boosts.
- Not ported: allies / rampart opening, nukes, quad breaker and decorative walls.
