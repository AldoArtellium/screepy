# Decisions: resources

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

## Terminal, minerals and market (`src/core/resources.ts`, Hivemind `room.resources.ts`, `resource-level-manager.ts`, `funnel-manager.ts`, rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-endgame.md`)

- Minerals: spawn role `mineralHarvester` (priority 3, weight 0.2) with an active extractor, its container, minerals left, 5000 effective energy and the room not full on minerals (> 1/3 of storage + terminal capacity); the creep (`roles/mineralHarvester.ts`) stands on the mineral container, harvests when the extractor is ready, recycles once the mineral is empty. Deviation: a role of its own (Hivemind: `harvester` with `fixedMineralSource`).
- Dispatcher: container source also offers non-energy resources (priority 3; the mineral container's mineral from half full); storage source and destination pick storage or terminal (`bestStorageSource` / `bestStorageTarget`: energy to the terminal below 7000, else where the resource fills the smaller share of the free space); storage source empties a terminal over 80% full; terminal destination: energy up to 5000 (priority 3) and `room.memory.fillTerminal` (priority 4, a send or a deal waiting for the resource). Transporters stay delivering while they carry non-energy.
- `TerminalTransfers`: resource levels (`resourceLevel`, energy `[200k, 50k, 20k]`, more for rooms with enemies or funnel targets, other resources `[50k, 0, 0]`), a high/excessive room sends 5000 to a low one (or medium from excessive), one send per room and run; funnel targets (`roomsToFunnel`) pull energy.
- `MarketManager` (Hivemind trade.ts): base minerals per room with a terminal: > 50k instant sell, > 20k sell order (Hivemind also buys below 10k / 5k: replaced on 2026-11-04 by the lab demand buys below); compounds sold above 5k when the market pays more than their reagents; power, ops; overflowing terminals; prices from history + order book, 10000 credits kept, orders cancelled after 100k ticks. Deviations: at most 10 deals per tick, no deal from a terminal on cooldown or already used this tick, spent credits deducted within a run. Commodities and deposit resources (bars, battery, wire, silicon...: loot, nothing uses them without a factory) are all sold, instant sell and a sell order (`isCommodity`, not in Hivemind). Price rules in `market/pricing.ts` (offline check `node tests/market/run.mjs`, in CI), not in Hivemind (shard0 2026-10-25: ~8M credits earned selling H at ~600, ~11.5M spent force-buying O / L / Z / U / X for the labs, then 21 credits left and an H sell order created at 627.86 stuck above competitors at 620.30, never repriced, so nothing sold, nothing could be bought and W29N7's LHO2 + X reaction waited): a surplus goes to the best buy order when it pays at least what a sell order would ask (H's history average, 257, is dragged down by big low-price volumes: a new order asked at most 465 while buy orders paid 562), or while credits are below the 10000 reserve (then at the average, Hivemind's average + dev / 2 otherwise); an own sell order undercut by a competitor is lowered to 1% below it (never below average + dev / 2, never raised: a raise costs a fee, at most once per 1000 ticks per order, heap); forced buys pay at most 1.2 x the higher of the average and the best buy order (Hivemind: any price; L was bought at 725-770; with 1.5 and the deploy's 3.4M credits, X was bought at 851-1100 for an average of 793, ~6.4M credits). Not measured in the mockup (no NPC orders): confirm on shard0 with the money history (`/api/user/money-history`) and `/api/game/market/my-orders`. Not ported: energy buying (off in Hivemind), pixels, labs/boosts, factory, power processing.
- Base mineral buys follow the lab demand (2026-11-04, not in Hivemind): shard0 money history over 21k ticks (to tick 78794144): H sold +37.6M, bought O -17.0M (~560 a unit), X -12.3M (~886), U -4.2M, L -1.8M, Z -1.7M, K -0.2M, fees -2.1M, net -1.8M, credits down to 0.30M, while `boosted:GH` was 18.5 parts per 1000 ticks and `Memory.boost` empty: the 10k / 5k per room floor bought reagents for stocks nothing used. Now `labs/chain.ts` `mineralNeeds` pushes each demand target's chain deficit (`chainDeficits`, against storage + terminal of every terminal room plus labs and carriers of reaction rooms) down to base minerals: a military demand (`demandTargets` kind `military`: an attack mission or NORMAL+ enemies at home) is force-bought (same 1.2 x cap), an upgrade compound only by a buy order at most its worth (`upgradeValuePerMineral`: the extra progress of a boosted WORK over a whole life per base mineral, GH 5, GH2O 5.7, XGH2O 6.25 energy, x the energy price: ~45-55 credits at shard0's ~8.7, against 500-900 for a base mineral, so never at today's prices; the energy price is only read once an upgrade target lacks a base mineral), a threat or defense stock (kind `stock`) never; needs below 500 are ignored and our buy orders for a mineral without need are cancelled (the open orders of the old rule too); for a mineral an upgrade compound still needs, our buy orders priced above its worth are cancelled too (`market/pricing.ts` `buyOrdersAbovePrice`; the old rule's orders sat near the market average, O ~560, and `tryBuy` never makes a second order, so they would have kept filling until 100k ticks old), a new one at the capped price follows on the next run. Selling is unchanged. Offline check `node tests/boosts/run.mjs` (demand targets, worth, needs). Not measured in the mockup (no NPC orders): confirm with the money history after the deploy (O / X / U / L / Z / K buys should stop while `Memory.military.auto = false` and no mission).
- Mockup: `--preset rcl6` (`rcl5`..`rcl8`) fast-forwards the room once the bot stored its plan (controller level, planned storage with 300k energy, extensions, towers, containers, roads, main ramparts at 600k); the log adds `endgame` structure counts, terminal energy and minerals by holder. Measured (W7N3, rcl6, 6000 ticks): terminal and extractor built by tick ~1700, minerals flowing from tick ~2900 (L in container, storage and terminal: ~8.7k at 6000), terminal energy held at 5-8k, 2 labs built (third under way) once the ramparts held; same CPU as before (14.5 vs 14.3, official estimate 17.8 both), no module error; RCL 6 progress 157k vs 261k (the 255k energy of terminal, extractor and labs goes to construction instead of upgrading).

## Labs and boosts (`src/labs/`, Hivemind `labs*.ts`, `boost-manager.ts`, `role/helper.ts`, `dispatcher/*/lab.ts`; targeting from The International `labs.ts`; rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-labs.md`)

- Layout (`reactions.ts`, Hivemind labs.position): with 3+ active labs, the 2 source labs within range 2 of the most other labs (`room.memory.labs = {source1, source2, reactor[]}`), recomputed when the lab count changes or every 3000 ticks.
- Reaction choice: The International's reverse chain (deviation: Hivemind makes whatever pair of reagents it has most of, T1 capped at 10k), pure logic in `chain.ts` (offline check `node tests/boosts/run.mjs`, in CI). Targets per room (`reactionTargets`), 3000 each: the upgrade compound (GH at RCL 6, GH2O at RCL 7: one reactor at RCL 6 makes GH ~2.4x faster than GH2O), then the military ones XLHO2 (HEAL), XGHO2 (TOUGH), XUH2O (ATTACK), XKHO2 (RANGED_ATTACK), XLH2O (WORK repair) only while `roomThreat` > 0 and automatic attacks are on (`Memory.military.auto !== false`, since 2026-11-04: no squad uses that stock otherwise; it is never bought, see the market), before the upgrade compound during an attack mission (`currentTarget`) or with NORMAL+ enemies at home. Otherwise (no threat, or `auto = false`, shard0's setting since 2026-11-04) a defense stock comes after the upgrade compound (`chain.ts` `DEFENSE_TARGETS`, kind `stock`: never bought, made only from minerals the rooms hold), since defense draws on the same compounds whatever the flag: guardians (ATTACK or RANGED_ATTACK) and emergency builders (WORK repair) against NORMAL+ enemies at home (`spawnRoles.ts` roomDefense `boostsFor`), boosted remote defenders against boosted invaders (`remoteDefense.ts` `boostStock` / `boostedResponse`: one compound must cover every part of an effect; shard0 2026-10-26 W29N6: a mini brawler with UH2O on 5 ATTACK). Sized to one response at the largest bodies (`core/bodyBuilder`, RCL 7-8 at 90% of the capacity): XUH2O 990 (a melee guardian's 33 ATTACK; mini brawler 5), XKHO2 1020 (blinky pair 2 x 17 RANGED_ATTACK; ranged guardian 33), XLHO2 480 (blinky pair 2 x 8 HEAL), XLH2O 510 (builder 17 WORK), XGHO2 240 (TOUGH blinky pair 2 x 4, RCL 6-7); ~17k base minerals per room at tier 3 (the 3000 threat stock: ~75k), tier 2 without X; each reaction batch still makes at least 1000. Without it (first version of the 2026-11-04 change) the threat stock was the only one and stopped with `auto = false`, so defense would have found no compound once the old stock was used. Mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl8 --labs --minerals H:20000,X:10000` (L from the extractor): the room now makes LH towards XLH2O (`reaction:LH` 320 / 160 in 3000 ticks), no module error. No MOVE compound (nothing uses it). Before (2026-10-25) the military list always came first: shard0 W27N5 / W29N7 made UH, UH2O, OH, LO, LH, GHO2 for 20000 ticks without a single boost. Deficits are pushed down to the reagents (`chainDecompose`); the first target in deficit whose reagents are there (a quarter of the deficit + 5% per reactor) is made, else its first missing reagent that can be made, recursively (base minerals are bought by the `MarketManager` only for a demand since 2026-11-04, see the market; Hivemind: below 10k / 5k per room). Not in TI: a reagent held by another room with a terminal counts as there (`importableFrom`; the terminal transfers then pull the reagents of the reaction under way), except a reagent of that room's own reaction (low there, never sent: two rooms both on G would wait for each other); shard0 has Z/K in W29N7 and U/L in W27N5, so each room stopped at ZK or UL. `room.memory.currentReaction` + `reactionTarget` (stock + the deficit, between 1000 and 3000; TI: at most 5000, no minimum); replaced when a reagent runs out or the target is reached (checked every 50 ticks), at the latest after 1500 ticks. Without X the chain stops at tier 2 compounds.
- Reactions: every reactor not reserved for boosts runs once the source labs hold the reagents (counters `reaction:<compound>`, `lab:waiting`: ticks waited, counted per check since 2026-11-04).
- Labs CPU (2026-11-04, not in Hivemind; shard0: Labs 0.67 CPU per tick for 5 rooms with labs, run every tick, avgCpu 1.0): `operationalLabs` skips `isActive()` (the engine's `checkStructureAgainstController` walks every object of the room for each lab) when the room's controller is ours and the room has no more labs than its RCL allows, the engine's own answer then (any other controller: `isActive()`); and `labsModule` skips a room until `manageReactions` says a lab can act (the shortest reactor cooldown or the product's `REACTION_TIME` after a reaction, 5 ticks while source labs wait for reagents or a reactor fails, at most the next 50-tick finished-reaction check, 50 ticks without labs or reaction), except while a boost is pending (`Memory.boost` creeps or labs: every room, every tick, like before). Mockup W7N3, before -> after: `--preset rcl8 --labs --minerals H:20000,X:10000` 3000 ticks (10 labs, nothing to make): Labs 0.686 -> 0.118 CPU per tick with the isActive shortcut alone, 0.064 / 0.066 with both (2 runs); `--preset plan7 --labs --minerals H:20000,O:10000,G:3000` 6000 ticks (6 labs, OH then GH, upgrader boosts), 2 runs each: Labs 0.561 / 0.601 -> 0.136 / 0.150, helper 0.171 / 0.142 -> 0.050 / 0.065 (its boost lab lookups use `operationalLabs` too), same chemistry (`reaction:GH` 2100 both -> 2095 both, `reaction:OH` 2420 / 2400 -> 2460 / 2395, `boosted:GH` 70 -> 69, `boost:done` 2 -> 2), RCL 7 progress 275k / 278k -> 284k / 277k; `--preset rcl8 --labs` with base minerals, X and 3000 XUH2O / XLHO2 / XGHO2, `--enemy W8N3:3`, attack set at tick 1, 4000 ticks: Labs 1.022 -> 0.199, `reaction:KO` 3080 both, `reaction:OH` 1240 -> 3455, duo boosted (`boosted:XLHO2` 25, `boost:done` 1) both, no module error. With the defense stock (`--preset rcl8 --labs --minerals H:20000,X:10000`, 3000 ticks, single runs on a loaded host, CPU per tick): Labs 0.831 (ccc75e2) -> 0.053 (lab demand without defense stock) -> 0.085 / 0.062 (defense stock: the room now makes LH, `lab:waiting` 30 / 35); MarketManager 0.055 -> 0.045 -> 0.062 / 0.031, within the host noise (average CPU 17.5 / 14.1 / 17.0 / 15.1 for the same room): the mockup has no market orders and RCL 8 no upgrade target, so the lab needs return before any price lookup.
- Transport: dispatcher `lab` source (reactor products 0..3 by fullness, 3 when not the current product, wrong or unneeded reagents in source labs 3) and destination (reagent to its source lab, 4 below LAB_REACTION_AMOUNT, 3, -1 above 20%); transporters fetch the reagent from storage / terminal (Hivemind).
- Boosts (`boosts.ts`): a spawn role's optional `boosts(room, option, body)` gives a compound per part type (best effect with enough stock for every part, beyond what other creeps asked; TOUGH: lowest damage multiplier, Hivemind's getBestBoost took the highest), the SpawnManager marks the creep (`Memory.boost.creeps[name] = {boosts: {compound: parts}, until}`); each requested compound gets a lab (never a source lab; no longer a reactor); `CreepRunner` walks the creep to the best prepared lab and boosts it once the lab holds compound and energy for all its parts. Deviation: the request expires 150 ticks after the creep leaves the spawn (Hivemind: 70% of its life, ~450 ticks), so squads never wait long. Users: military squads (TOUGH, ATTACK, HEAL, RANGED_ATTACK; MOVE stays plain, bodies are sized for plain MOVE), guardians and emergency builders (Defense), upgraders at RCL 6-7. Duos and quads stay `forming` while a member is being boosted. The choice is `boostChoice.ts` `pickBoost`; a plan gives compound and parts per part type, `boostCreep(creep, parts)` boosts exactly those.
- Upgrader boosts (not in Hivemind, which boosts upgraders at RCL 8 only to save WORK parts): a boosted WORK upgrades x1.5 / x1.8 / x2 for the same energy and CPU. Partial boosts (`minParts` 5: as many WORK as the stock allows, the biggest total gain), and the spawn role's `boostWhileWorking`: the request (`working`, timeout 400 ticks after the spawn) only overrides the upgrader once a boost lab holds the compound and energy, so it never waits idle. Not at RCL 8 (15 energy per tick cap: needs smaller boosted bodies). Mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl6 --labs`, single runs: 6000 ticks with 3000 GH seeded, RCL 6 progress 290k -> 359k (+24%), same upgrade energy (272k / 273k), 6 boosts (110 parts), CPU 13.2 both; from base minerals (H 16k, O 7k, U 5k, L 3k, K 8k, Z 8k), 15000 ticks: the chain made ZK, UL, G, then GH from tick ~5500, progress 659k -> 704k (+6.9%; ticks 9500-15000 +15%), 5 boosts (68 parts), 4 helpers, CPU 8.9 both, no module error.
- Helper (`roles/helper.ts`, spawn role `helper`, priority 5, weight 1.1, CARRY up to 12): one while the room has a boost lab; empties boost labs holding another mineral, brings each its compound and energy, parks on `helper_parking`. Deviation: after 100 ticks without boost lab it stores its cargo and recycles (Hivemind parks it for its whole life; upgraders are boosted once per life and a parked helper cost ~0.8 CPU per tick).
- Resource levels (`core/resources.ts`, Hivemind): boost compounds `[15k, 0, 0]` (`[15k, 7.5k, 2.5k]` for attack, ranged and repair under attack, upgrade at RCL 8), reagents of the reaction under way `[30k, 20k, 10k]`: terminal sends follow.
- Mockup: `--labs` (plan presets) builds the plan's terminal and labs at once, `--minerals H:20000,X:10000,...` adds resources to the preset storage; the log adds lab contents (`lab:<resource>`) and `boosted` (boosted parts of our creeps in the room by role and compound).
- Measured (W7N3, `--preset rcl8 --labs`, base minerals + 3000 XUH2O / XLHO2 / XGHO2 seeded, `--enemy W8N3:3`, attack set at tick 1): the chain made 3000 KO (ticks 11-1250), then LO, OH, LHO2 and XLHO2 by tick 3600 (8 reactors); the duo was boosted (XLHO2, XUH2O) by tick 772, the forced quad (XGHO2, XUH2O, XLHO2, KO for the ranged parts) between ticks 747 and 926; the RCL 3 base fell without loss both times; no module error, code CPU 11.6-12.4 (helper ~0.8 per tick while alive).
- Not done: squad sizing (`sizing.ts`) ignores boosts, MOVE boosts (needs bodies with fewer MOVE), RCL 8 upgrader / mineral harvester boosts (remote defenders: see Remote defense, boosted only when they win no other way), boost labs prepared before the spawn, compound recovery (recycle / unboost), evacuation.

Counters in `Memory.stats.counters`: `cpu:module:<name>`, `spawned:<role>`, `work:{harvest,build,repair,upgrade,builderUpgrade,remoteHarvest,remoteBuild,remoteRepair,pioneerHarvest,pioneerBuild,pioneerUpgrade}` (all in energy: harvested = WORK x HARVEST_POWER capped by the source energy; spent on build = WORK x BUILD_POWER capped by the energy carried and the progress left, upgrade = WORK x UPGRADE_CONTROLLER_POWER capped by the energy carried, repair = hits repaired x REPAIR_COST rounded up; `utils/history.ts` `countWork` and its `*Energy` helpers; before 2026-10-21 harvest counted WORK parts and the others calls), `work:mineral` (mineral units), `work:dismantle` (hits), `builder:fetch` (builder ticks spent fetching, was `work:builderFetch`), `deliver:<order type>`, `idle:<role>`, `harvester:{walking,delivering}`, `spawn:starved`, `roleError:<role>`, `moveError:<code>:<role>`, `history:{snapshot,trimmed}`. Room counters (heap, flushed into the history, see Memory): `harvest`, `remoteHarvest`, `build`, `repair`, `upgrade` (energy, by the creep's home room), `spawn:<role>` (spawn energy), `idle:<role>` and `builder:fetch` (ticks).
