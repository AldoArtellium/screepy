# Decisions: expansion

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

## Expansion (`src/expansion/`, Hivemind `process/strategy/expand.ts`, `builder.remote`, rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-expansion.md`)

- When: a room at RCL 5+ (where the squad spawns; Hivemind: 4, which searched targets with no origin), fewer owned rooms than the GCL, the CPU average (`Memory.remoteMining.cpuAvg`, every tick over ~400 ticks; else `Memory.stats.cpuAvg`) below the CPU limit with one room, else Hivemind's `getCpuStats`: below 80% of it with fewer than 5 remote rooms, below the whole limit from 5 (`remoteIncome.ts` `expansionCpuFits`, checked by `tests/remoteIncome/run.mjs`; the remote budget then drops remote rooms above 95%). Before (2026-10-25), 80% as soon as we owned 2 rooms: shard0 (8 remote rooms, 17-19 of 20 CPU, the budget filling up to 95%) could never expand. Not measured in the mockup (its 3x3 fixtures leave no expansion target). Target: best expansion score (`expansionScore.ts`: sources, new mineral, remote sources around, shared remotes, exit/wall/swamp tiles, distance > 3 rooms; cached 20000 ticks) among scouted rooms nobody owns, lowered near past failures, reachable in <= 10 rooms from an RCL 5+ room with a spawn; no target: next search in 500 ticks.
- Squad `expand` (`Memory.expand.squads`, spawn role `squad`, priority 4 above 10000 effective energy, 2 from 5000, weight 1.1): a claimer (1 CLAIM, swamp moves; attacks hostile reservations), 2 pioneers, a defender while the target has hostiles. Support squads: 1 pioneer from every other RCL 4+ room with 50000 energy. Each squad costs its spawn room one remote room until the target is claimed (`claimingSquadsOf`; deviation: Hivemind's roomNeeds counts every squad, so after the claim the 2 pioneers, kept for up to 30000 ticks, still cost a whole remote room: shard0 W29N7 mined 2 remote rooms instead of 3 while W28N7 grew). Mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl5 --cpu-limit 300`, expansion seeded in Memory, 1500 ticks: 4 remote rooms (8 sources) with the target claimed, 3 (6) while claiming.
- Pioneers (`builder.remote`): fill up at home (if >= 3000 energy), then live in the target: upgrade to RCL 2 first, fill spawn and towers, build towers > spawn > closest site, get energy from piles, storage, containers or by harvesting. The new room plans its variations and builds its spawn from the final plan; it mines no remote before its spawn.
- Ends with RCL 4 + storage (success), or after 7500 ticks unclaimed / 30000 ticks after the claim (failure, remembered 1M ticks). Reclaim (spawn role `reclaim`, priority 3): an owned room without spawn (not the target, >= 2 rooms) gets up to 5 pioneers from rooms with 10000 energy and bucket >= 8000.
- Deviations: units spawn claimer first (Hivemind: random), the defender only against hostiles (Hivemind: always one), the claim happens on arrival; no exit direction is safe in the score. Not ported: tunnels to the controller, evacuation, terminal help (RCL 6).
- Mockup: `--gcl 2` allows a second room; the report lists `world.ownedRooms`. `--claim ROOM[:rcl]` owns a neighbour room from tick 1 (controller at that RCL, default 1, and a spawn at the harness spawn spot, GCL raised to 2), like a room the expansion just claimed: the fixture's 3x3 rooms leave no expansion target, this is how two homes sharing remotes are tested (`--preset rcl5 --claim W8N3:2`).
