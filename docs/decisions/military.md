# Decisions: military

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

Since 2026-10-27 the ROI cost is the squad's spawn energy and boosts with replacements (`roi.ts` `squadCost`): the CPU price under pressure and the spawn time cost described in the Target bullet below are gone, and so are the opt-in raids.

## Military (`src/military/`, our own design, milestone 7 steps 1-2)

- Target: `Memory.military.attack = "<room>"` (by hand), else an automatic target (`targets.ts`, every 100 ticks) by return on investment in energy (`roi.ts`, offline check `node tests/militaryRoi/run.mjs`, in CI): value = loot seen in the room (`intel.ts` records `loot`: containers, ruins, others' storage and terminal, each resource at its market price in energy, `MarketManager.energyPrice`, 1 without price history) + for a stronghold the remote income it blocks (3 energy per tick per source of its room until its collapse, at most 10000 ticks) + for a player the energy they made us lose (infamy, counted only for an aggressor, see Strategic reasons) and the remote sources its room's fall frees (only as many as the remote budget can take) (`roi.ts` `freedSources`: the room's own sources and those of the rooms that player reserves whose strictly closest room of theirs is the target, reservations seen within 5000 ticks, each within 2 rooms of ours; 3 energy per tick per source over 10000 ticks; shard0 W28N8 of doorknobnob: its 2 sources and W27N7, next to W28N7 which mines one remote source); cost = spawn cost of the squad's bodies x 2 (replacements; `sizing.ts` `squadMemberCosts`), plus under CPU pressure (`remoteIncome.ts` `cpuPressure`: remote budget capped or CPU above 85%) the squad's CPU priced in energy: `squadCpu` = members x 2 x 160 official CPU (mockup `--preset rcl7 --stronghold W7N4:1`, 4000 ticks: ~1900 CPU for 12 quad creeps, the Military module carries the quad's actions) x `neutralEnergyPerCpu(50, capacity)` (8.4 at 1300 capacity, 11.2 at 5600): a quad at RCL 7 costs ~45% more, the level 1 stronghold blocking 2 sources still pays (ROI 1.30). The log prints the CPU part. Mockup: `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3` with `auto`: W8N3 chosen at tick 1700 for value 60000 (its 2 sources; before, value 0 and no target), tower destroyed by 3000; strongholds (`--cpu-limit 13` and `6`): W7N4 chosen at 1000 / 1100 as before (value 60000, cost 23040), CPU price 0 in both (the average stayed under 85% of the limit when the target was picked: the pricing is only checked offline). The cost also counts the income the squad's spawn time takes from the origin room (`roi.ts` `spawnTimeCost`: parts x 3 ticks x 2 x the room's harvest per tick (local + remote, history room counters over 5000 ticks) / its spawns x min(1, spawn busy / 0.8); shard0 W29N7, one spawn 88% busy, ~50 energy per tick: 33.6k for a 112-part quad, twice its spawn energy; W27N9's ROI 6.75 -> ~2.6), logged as `spawn time`. A squad's first member only spawns once the room's effective energy covers the whole squad + 2000, the next ones go back to back at priority 5 (above transporters; the W29N7 quad's healers waited 200+ ticks of their life for the attackers); mockup `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3` with the attack forced: same timeline (members already back to back on a quiet spawn), base destroyed at 1889 both. A target needs value / cost >= 1, the best wins, the current one counts 1.5 x its return and is kept whatever its return while its squad has creeps (spawning ones included) and it is still a candidate (`roi.ts` `pickTarget` `engaged`; shard0 W27N5 2026-10-25: a forming duo against W29N3 was recycled, its attacker boosted first, when W27N9 scored 6.75); a mission ended by a target change is logged `retargeted`, not `abandoned` (not left alone 20000 ticks), and every mission end drops its members' boost requests (`boosts.ts` `cancelBoost`: the CreepRunner boosts before the role recycles); `Memory.military.target.roi` and the log give value and cost. Candidates: strongholds (invader cores of level 1 to `maxStrongholdLevel`, default 1) within 2 rooms of our rooms or 1 of our remotes; player rooms unless `Memory.military.auto = false` (on by default since 2026-10-25, deployed on shard0 with doorknobnob's W28N8 / W27N9 in sight; before, off without opt-in; safe mode, rooms without spawn and failures of the last 20000 ticks skipped). Before (Harabi's durability x distance / threat, strongholds always first) a level 1 stronghold with nothing around was attacked for ~100 units of loot; now only when it blocks remote sources (ROI 0.003 alone, 1.9 with 2 sources and its collapse far away, 0.19 collapsing in 1000 ticks; mockup `--preset rcl7 --stronghold W7N4:1`: W7N4's 2 sources, value 60064 for a 23k quad, ROI 2.61, target at tick 1200 as before). Level 0 cores in our remotes stay with the remote defense ATTACKER, which now leaves strongholds (core level 1+) alone. The squad goes from the closest RCL 4+ room (`minRcl` to lower it), spawn role `military` (priority 3, effective energy >= 2000). Ends when the room has no spawn, tower or invader core left (success) or after 10 lost creeps.
- `intel.ts`: every 10 ticks, hostile rooms in sight (`Memory.military.rooms`: owner, RCL, towers, spawns, safe mode, weakest barrier, core level / deploy / collapse, creep damage and heal); infamy (`Memory.military.infamy`: energy value of each player's creeps seen in our rooms and remotes, x0.95 every 1000 ticks); aggression (`Memory.military.aggression`: hits of damage each player's creeps dealt to ours, our creeps and the structures of our owned and remote rooms, from `EVENT_ATTACK` in the room event log, our hits back on their melee creeps left out; `getEventLog` is only read in a room where armed player creeps (ATTACK, RANGED_ATTACK or WORK) stood the tick before, the attacker's owner and our creep ids taken from that tick so a dead attacker or victim still counts; x0.95 per 1000 elapsed ticks, `aggressionDecayed`). Unlike infamy, scouts, harvesters and reservers never raise it.

The API gives no other player's GCL, so a player's size is the number of distinct rooms seen owned by them within 50000 ticks (`Memory.military.rooms` owners and the static intel, freshest record per room: a lower bound). A player with more rooms than we own is not an automatic target unless its aggression reaches 3000 hits (`strategy.ts` `AGGRESSION_RETALIATE`, ~10 tower shots; 300 = `AGGRESSION_HOSTILE` for the others, see Strategic reasons). Equal counts are not stronger (mockup enemy bot: 1 room against our 1). `Memory.military.attack` is unchanged. Mockup (single runs, host overloaded, Military often skipped by the CPU limit): `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3`, 3000 ticks: no attack before or after (an unboosted squad has been infeasible against a player's towers since they count as refilled), 194.7k points both, no module error; `--preset rcl6 --labs --minerals GHO2:1200,UH2O:2000,XLHO2:1000 --enemy W8N3:3`, 4000 ticks: W8N3 still picked (tick 2000 before, 1000 after), spawn and tower destroyed by tick 3000 / 2000, no module error. - Strategic reasons (`strategy.ts`, pure, offline check in `tests/militaryRoi/run.mjs`; our design, 2026-10-26): an automatic target needs a reason that serves our growth, before any return on investment: `aggressor` (the player dealt us `AGGRESSION_HOSTILE` 300 hits, 3000 if it looks stronger), `expansion` (it claimed our expansion target `Memory.expand.current`, or reserves it from its strictly closest room; valued at least the target room's sources), `territory` (its fall frees remote sources the remote CPU budget can take: `spareRemoteRooms` = `maxRooms` - rooms mined, x2 sources per free room, 0 when the budget is full, any when uncapped), and for strongholds the same budget rule on their room's sources. Loot alone and infamy alone are no reason; infamy only adds to an aggressor's value. A stronger player is only attacked as an aggressor. The current target keeps its reason while its squad has creeps (no squad recycled when the budget fills up or aggression decays). Rooms left alone are listed in `Memory.military.skipped` (room: why, rebuilt every target search, logged on a change), printed by `live-inspect` (section Military with the target, aggression and missions of the last 50000 ticks) and in the report (`left alone`). shard0 before (tick 78586437): 5 missions in ~25000 ticks, 17 creeps lost, Blindfold's RCL 2 W25N7 destroyed (rebuilt since) and DoctorCat92's W29N3 for 300 loot, while no player had hurt us (aggression empty) and the remote budget held 6 of 6 rooms. - `sizing.ts` (TI combat requests): threat = towers x 600 (close) or x 150 (edge, range 20+) + enemy creep damage; duo when its healer covers the edge damage and its attacker beats the enemy heal x 1.2, else quad; `Memory.military.squad = "duo" | "quad"` forces it. - `combat.ts`: worst damage on a tile next tick (every enemy tower focusing, melee within 2, ranged within 4) vs our healing; verdict safe (healing outpaces damage), rescue (survives 3 ticks), doomed.
- `duo.ts`: forming -> advance -> retreat (below 60% hits) -> advance (from 95%). The attacker only steps with the healer next to it; nobody steps onto a tile that kills it within 3 ticks, evaluated on the tile it really lands on (exit tiles carry creeps to the next room); when only the healer would be doomed the attacker goes in alone and comes back to be healed, which drains towers. In the room: towers, the invader core, spawns, then other structures, breaking the weakest ramparts on the way (path cost by hits). Trace: `[Duo]` log lines.
- `quad.ts`: 2 attackers (TOUGH + ATTACK) and 2 healers (HEAL, a RANGED_ATTACK per 3 HEAL), `memory.quad` / `memory.slot`. forming (home, refilled there when a healer or two members are lost) -> travel (loose chain, the leader waits for the others) -> engage: pack into a free 2x2 block a tile away from exits, then path the top-left tile on a 2x2 cost matrix (each tile = most expensive tile of its block, a structure costs the ticks the quad needs to break it, 255 where the block touches an exit) and move all four in the same direction; nobody moves while one is fatigued; a rampart or wall in the next block is broken first. Border drain first (state `drain`, towers seen or never seen): one attacker at a time (most hits, from 90%) stands on the exit tile and bounces between the rooms (a creep left on an exit tile changes room every tick), the towers spend a shot on it every other tick, the others heal it from the rally point; it steps back below 60%; over once the towers are seen empty (again after a retreat). Inside, while towers with energy out-damage our healing on the next step, the quad goes no closer and soaks shots on the block around (range 8) with the least tower damage. Retreat (as a packed block while 3+ tiles from the border, then each to a rally point 3 tiles inside the staging room; loose retreats left a member to die alone) when a member drops below 60% or could not walk to the closest exit under the net damage (+3 ticks); back from 95%. While holding, single swaps of two members: attackers next to the focus, the member with the most hits on the tile the towers and hostiles hit (they shoot the closest). Healing: each healer heals the member with the lowest expected hits (hits minus the damage expected on its tile), then counts its heal in (pre-heal). Attackers hit an adjacent fighter, the focus, or the weakest adjacent hostile structure; healers' ranged parts pick a mass attack when it beats a single shot. Trace: `[Quad]` log lines (slot, position, hits of each member, action).
