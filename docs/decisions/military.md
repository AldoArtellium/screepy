# Decisions: military

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

Since 2026-10-27 the ROI cost is the squad's spawn energy and boosts with replacements (`roi.ts` `squadCost`): the CPU price under pressure and the spawn time cost described in the Target bullet below are gone, and so are the opt-in raids.

## Military (`src/military/`, our own design, milestone 7 steps 1-2)

- Target: `Memory.military.attack = "<room>"` (by hand), else an automatic target (`targets.ts`, every 100 ticks) by return on investment in energy (`roi.ts`, offline check `node tests/militaryRoi/run.mjs`, in CI): value = loot seen in the room (`intel.ts` records `loot`: containers, ruins, others' storage and terminal, each resource at its market price in energy, `MarketManager.energyPrice`, 1 without price history) + for a stronghold the remote income it blocks (3 energy per tick per source of its room until its collapse, at most 10000 ticks) + for a player the energy they made us lose (infamy, counted only for an aggressor, see Strategic reasons) and the remote sources its room's fall frees (only as many as the remote budget can take) (`roi.ts` `freedSources`: the room's own sources and those of the rooms that player reserves whose strictly closest room of theirs is the target, reservations seen within 5000 ticks, each within 2 rooms of ours; 3 energy per tick per source over 10000 ticks; shard0 W28N8 of doorknobnob: its 2 sources and W27N7, next to W28N7 which mines one remote source); cost = spawn cost of the squad's bodies x 2 (replacements; `sizing.ts` `squadMemberCosts`), plus under CPU pressure (`remoteIncome.ts` `cpuPressure`: remote budget capped or CPU above 85%) the squad's CPU priced in energy: `squadCpu` = members x 2 x 160 official CPU (mockup `--preset rcl7 --stronghold W7N4:1`, 4000 ticks: ~1900 CPU for 12 quad creeps, the Military module carries the quad's actions) x `neutralEnergyPerCpu(50, capacity)` (8.4 at 1300 capacity, 11.2 at 5600): a quad at RCL 7 costs ~45% more, the level 1 stronghold blocking 2 sources still pays (ROI 1.30). The log prints the CPU part. Mockup: `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3` with `auto`: W8N3 chosen at tick 1700 for value 60000 (its 2 sources; before, value 0 and no target), tower destroyed by 3000; strongholds (`--cpu-limit 13` and `6`): W7N4 chosen at 1000 / 1100 as before (value 60000, cost 23040), CPU price 0 in both (the average stayed under 85% of the limit when the target was picked: the pricing is only checked offline). The cost also counts the income the squad's spawn time takes from the origin room (`roi.ts` `spawnTimeCost`: parts x 3 ticks x 2 x the room's harvest per tick (local + remote, history room counters over 5000 ticks) / its spawns x min(1, spawn busy / 0.8); shard0 W29N7, one spawn 88% busy, ~50 energy per tick: 33.6k for a 112-part quad, twice its spawn energy; W27N9's ROI 6.75 -> ~2.6), logged as `spawn time`. A squad's first member only spawns once the room's effective energy covers the whole squad + 2000, the next ones go back to back at priority 5 (above transporters; the W29N7 quad's healers waited 200+ ticks of their life for the attackers); mockup `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3` with the attack forced: same timeline (members already back to back on a quiet spawn), base destroyed at 1889 both. A target needs value / cost >= 1, the best wins, the current one counts 1.5 x its return and is kept whatever its return while its squad has creeps (spawning ones included) and it is still a candidate (`roi.ts` `pickTargets`, its `engaged` list; shard0 W27N5 2026-10-25: a forming duo against W29N3 was recycled, its attacker boosted first, when W27N9 scored 6.75); a mission ended by a target change is logged `retargeted`, not `abandoned` (not left alone 20000 ticks), and every mission end drops its members' boost requests (`boosts.ts` `cancelBoost`: the CreepRunner boosts before the role recycles); `Memory.military.targets[].roi` and the log give value and cost. Candidates: strongholds (invader cores of level 1 to `maxStrongholdLevel`, default 1) within 2 rooms of our rooms or 1 of our remotes; player rooms unless `Memory.military.auto = false` (on by default since 2026-10-25, deployed on shard0 with doorknobnob's W28N8 / W27N9 in sight; before, off without opt-in; safe mode, rooms without spawn and failures of the last 20000 ticks skipped). Before (Harabi's durability x distance / threat, strongholds always first) a level 1 stronghold with nothing around was attacked for ~100 units of loot; now only when it blocks remote sources (ROI 0.003 alone, 1.9 with 2 sources and its collapse far away, 0.19 collapsing in 1000 ticks; mockup `--preset rcl7 --stronghold W7N4:1`: W7N4's 2 sources, value 60064 for a 23k quad, ROI 2.61, target at tick 1200 as before). Level 0 cores in our remotes stay with the remote defense ATTACKER, which now leaves strongholds (core level 1+) alone. The squad goes from the closest RCL 4+ room (`minRcl` to lower it), spawn role `military` (priority 3, effective energy >= 2000). Ends when the room has no spawn, tower or invader core left (success) or after 10 lost creeps.
- `intel.ts`: every 10 ticks, hostile rooms in sight (`Memory.military.rooms`: owner, RCL, towers, spawns, safe mode, weakest barrier, core level / deploy / collapse, creep damage and heal); infamy (`Memory.military.infamy`: energy value of each player's creeps seen in our rooms and remotes, x0.95 every 1000 ticks); aggression (`Memory.military.aggression`: hits of damage each player's creeps dealt to ours, our creeps and the structures of our owned and remote rooms, from `EVENT_ATTACK` in the room event log, our hits back on their melee creeps left out; `getEventLog` is only read in a room where armed player creeps (ATTACK, RANGED_ATTACK or WORK) stood the tick before, the attacker's owner and our creep ids taken from that tick so a dead attacker or victim still counts; x0.95 per 1000 elapsed ticks, `aggressionDecayed`). Unlike infamy, scouts, harvesters and reservers never raise it.

The API gives no other player's GCL, so a player's size is the number of distinct rooms seen owned by them within 50000 ticks (`Memory.military.rooms` owners and the static intel, freshest record per room: a lower bound). A player with more rooms than we own is not an automatic target unless its aggression reaches 3000 hits (`strategy.ts` `AGGRESSION_RETALIATE`, ~10 tower shots; 300 = `AGGRESSION_HOSTILE` for the others, see Strategic reasons). Equal counts are not stronger (mockup enemy bot: 1 room against our 1). `Memory.military.attack` is unchanged. Mockup (single runs, host overloaded, Military often skipped by the CPU limit): `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3`, 3000 ticks: no attack before or after (an unboosted squad has been infeasible against a player's towers since they count as refilled), 194.7k points both, no module error; `--preset rcl6 --labs --minerals GHO2:1200,UH2O:2000,XLHO2:1000 --enemy W8N3:3`, 4000 ticks: W8N3 still picked (tick 2000 before, 1000 after), spawn and tower destroyed by tick 3000 / 2000, no module error. Aggression itself (a player hurting us) is only checked offline: the mockup enemy never enters our rooms.
- Strategic reasons (`strategy.ts`, pure, offline check in `tests/militaryRoi/run.mjs`; our design, 2026-10-26): an automatic target needs a reason that serves our growth, before any return on investment: `aggressor` (the player dealt us `AGGRESSION_HOSTILE` 300 hits, 3000 if it looks stronger), `expansion` (it claimed our expansion target `Memory.expand.current`, or reserves it from its strictly closest room; valued at least the target room's sources), `territory` (its fall frees remote sources the remote CPU budget can take: `spareRemoteRooms` = `maxRooms` - rooms mined, x2 sources per free room, 0 when the budget is full, any when uncapped), and for strongholds the same budget rule on their room's sources. Loot alone and infamy alone are no reason; infamy only adds to an aggressor's value. A stronger player is only attacked as an aggressor. The current target keeps its reason while its squad has creeps (no squad recycled when the budget fills up or aggression decays). Rooms left alone are listed in `Memory.military.skipped` (room: why, rebuilt every target search, logged on a change), printed by `live-inspect` (section Military with the target, aggression and missions of the last 50000 ticks) and in the report (`left alone`). shard0 before (tick 78586437): 5 missions in ~25000 ticks, 17 creeps lost, Blindfold's RCL 2 W25N7 destroyed (rebuilt since) and DoctorCat92's W29N3 for 300 loot, while no player had hurt us (aggression empty) and the remote budget held 6 of 6 rooms. Mockup (single runs, 3 in parallel): `--preset rcl6 --labs --minerals GHO2:1200,UH2O:2000,XLHO2:1000 --enemy W8N3:3`, 4000 ticks: W8N3 picked at 1000 (`territory`, value 60000 for 28851), destroyed at 1948, no loss; the same with `--memory '{"remoteMining":{"sources":{},"lastSelect":0,"maxRooms":0}}'`, 3000 ticks: `W8N3 left alone: enemy: no strategic reason (aggression 0, freed sources 2, usable 0)`, no attack (the budget rose to 1 then 2 rooms, both mined); `--preset rcl7 --stronghold W7N4:1`, 3000 ticks: W7N4 picked at 1000 (`stronghold`), destroyed at 2915, 4 lost; no module error (the tick-1 `"undefined" is not valid JSON` console error of the rcl6 preset is on master too).
- Encroachers (`strategy.ts` `isEncroacher`, our design, 2026-10-27; offline checks in `tests/militaryRoi/run.mjs`): shard0 W24N8 sits in a respawn area (until 2026-11-24) where its only reachable remote is W24N7, reserved and mined by Blindfold (GCL 8, 8 rooms) from W25N7 (RCL 3, rebuilt after our duo razed it at 78579596, no answer from Blindfold since). Feasibility is unchanged: a player's tower counts as refilled, so an unboosted squad only goes once the room has no tower (a rebuild) or with boosts in stock; W25N7 with its tower is not picked automatically. On a success against a player (`military.ts` `finish`, `targets.ts` `releaseFallen`), the room and the rooms they reserved from it (`roi.ts` `releasedRooms`) go to `Memory.remoteReleased[room] = { player, since, until }` for `RELEASE_TTL` 20000 ticks (`remote/remoteRooms.ts` `releaseFrom`, counter `remote:released`): their contest entries there are dropped, their reservation neither contests (RoomIntel) nor blocks (`isRemoteBlocked`), their creeps are ignored for `RELEASE_CREEP_GRACE` 1500 ticks (leftovers), and the room stays reserved under CPU pressure (`remoteSpawnRole.ts` `reserverOption`; our reserver already attacks another reservation). Counter `military:target:<reason>` per automatic target. Squad routes and remote paths never enter a room of another zone (`routeModel.ts` `routeRoomCost(..., sameZone)`, `RemoteMining.ts` `pathFor`, `utils/rooms.ts` `roomStatus`): findRoute ignores the respawn area walls, and the first manual attack on W25N7 staged in W25N8 behind them. - `sizing.ts` (TI combat requests): threat = towers x 600 (close) or x 150 (edge, range 20+) + enemy creep damage; duo when its healer covers the edge damage and its attacker beats the enemy heal x 1.2, else quad; `Memory.military.squad = "duo" | "quad"` forces it. Automatic targets only when feasible: the front creep lasts 20 ticks of net edge damage, or 10 ticks of net close damage against towers that refill (strongholds: so level 1 needs ~RCL 7, nothing below). A duo that lost a pair against a room now sized for a quad hands over to a quad. Boosts in the spawn room's stock count (`squadBoosts`, `planSquad(..., boostRoom)`; not in TI or Hivemind): the best compound `bestBoost` would give every HEAL, ATTACK and TOUGH part of the squad multiplies heal and damage, and boosted TOUGH counts 100 / damage-multiplier hits on the front creep. - `combat.ts`: worst damage on a tile next tick (every enemy tower focusing, melee within 2, ranged within 4) vs our healing; verdict safe (healing outpaces damage), rescue (survives 3 ticks), doomed.
- `duo.ts`: forming -> advance -> retreat (below 60% hits) -> advance (from 95%). The attacker only steps with the healer next to it; nobody steps onto a tile that kills it within 3 ticks, evaluated on the tile it really lands on (exit tiles carry creeps to the next room); when only the healer would be doomed the attacker goes in alone and comes back to be healed, which drains towers. In the room: towers, the invader core, spawns, then other structures, breaking the weakest ramparts on the way (path cost by hits). Trace: `[Duo]` log lines.
- `quad.ts`: 2 attackers (TOUGH + ATTACK) and 2 healers (HEAL, a RANGED_ATTACK per 3 HEAL), `memory.quad` / `memory.slot`. forming (home, refilled there when a healer or two members are lost) -> travel (loose chain, the leader waits for the others) -> engage: pack into a free 2x2 block a tile away from exits, then path the top-left tile on a 2x2 cost matrix (each tile = most expensive tile of its block, a structure costs the ticks the quad needs to break it, 255 where the block touches an exit) and move all four in the same direction; nobody moves while one is fatigued; a rampart or wall in the next block is broken first. Border drain first (state `drain`, towers seen or never seen): one attacker at a time (most hits, from 90%) stands on the exit tile and bounces between the rooms (a creep left on an exit tile changes room every tick), the towers spend a shot on it every other tick, the others heal it from the rally point; it steps back below 60%; over once the towers are seen empty (again after a retreat). Inside, while towers with energy out-damage our healing on the next step, the quad goes no closer and soaks shots on the block around (range 8) with the least tower damage. Retreat (as a packed block while 3+ tiles from the border, then each to a rally point 3 tiles inside the staging room; loose retreats left a member to die alone) when a member drops below 60% or could not walk to the closest exit under the net damage (+3 ticks); back from 95%. While holding, single swaps of two members: attackers next to the focus, the member with the most hits on the tile the towers and hostiles hit (they shoot the closest). Healing: each healer heals the member with the lowest expected hits (hits minus the damage expected on its tile), then counts its heal in (pre-heal). Attackers hit an adjacent fighter, the focus, or the weakest adjacent hostile structure; healers' ranged parts pick a mass attack when it beats a single shot. Trace: `[Quad]` log lines (slot, position, hits of each member, action).

## Controller takeover (2026-11-14, our design)

Problem (shard0, tick ~79037300, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-military-colony-takeover-design.md`): 10 missions, 37 creeps lost, 0 room gained. A "success" razed spawns and towers but never touched the controller, so the owner rebuilt (W25N7 razed twice is RCL 6 again, W29N4 RCL 7 with 3 towers after a success).

- Engine (screeps engine `attackController.js`, `controllers/tick.js`): attackController needs the creep adjacent, no safe mode and the upgrade block over; each CLAIM part takes CONTROLLER_CLAIM_DOWNGRADE 300 ticks off the downgrade timer and blocks upgrades (and safe mode activation, and our next attack) CONTROLLER_ATTACK_BLOCKED_UPGRADE 1000 ticks. When the timer runs out the level drops and CONTROLLER_DOWNGRADE[newLevel] / 2 is added to what is left (an overshoot carries over: one attack can drop two levels), safe modes go to 0; at level 0 the room is neutral. A CLAIM creep lives 600 ticks: one attacker per 1000-tick cycle.
- Pure model (`siegeModel.ts`, offline `tests/militarySiege/run.mjs`): `siegeClaimParts(capacity)` = min(25, floor(capacity / 650)); `siegeTicks(level, ticksToDowngrade, parts)` simulates an attack every 1000 ticks with the carry-over; `siegeCost(ticks, claimer, guard)` = ceil(ticks / 1000) claimers + ceil(ticks / 1500) guards; SIEGE_MAX_TICKS 60000. Full timers with 8 parts (RCL 7 base): RCL 2 5600 ticks, RCL 4 19000, RCL 5 36200, RCL 6 60000, RCL 7 over; with 19 parts RCL 4 9000; with 25 parts RCL 6 24000, RCL 8 49000.
- Intel (`HostileRoom`): `downgrade` (tick), `upgradeBlocked` (tick), `safeModeAvailable` for owned hostile controllers. `siegePlan` uses the timer seen at the record (`downgrade - t`: the owner keeps it topped up), the full timer without one.
- Takeover flag: a mission against a player's room (not Invader, not us) is a takeover when `Memory.military.takeover !== false` and the siege fits SIEGE_MAX_TICKS with the claimer of the mission's biggest base; decided at start, or once the intel has the room (a manual attack on a room never seen). Automatic targets (`targets.ts`): the cost adds `siegeEnergy` (claimers + guards, guard body at the biggest origin's capacity) when the return still holds at MIN_ROI with it (`AutoTargetEntry.takeover: true`); otherwise the target is attacked as before, squads only (`takeover: false`). Deviation from the spec (which added the siege to every takeover target's cost): a low RCL encroacher whose remote value can't pay a ~40-120k energy siege would otherwise stop being attacked at all.
- Takeover missions pass `{ takeover: true }` to `pickTarget` (duo and quad: the first barrier on the way to an enclosed controller once towers and spawns are gone), and `isNeutralized` also waits for `controllerBlocker` to be null from a squad member in the room.
- On success (`finish`): `Memory.military.sieges[room] = { owner, since, bases, rally, losses, attacks, lastAttack?, level?, downgrade?, blockedUntil?, guards? }`, at most `maxSieges` (default 2; over it the mission ends as before). Loot and `releaseFallen` as before. A sieged room is no automatic target.
- Siege creeps (`siege.ts` `siegeSpawnRole`, after `roomDefense` in ECONOMY_SPAWN_ROLES, priority 3 weight 1 like a squad's first member, base effective energy >= 2000): one `siegeGuard` from any base of the siege (ATTACK 4 : HEAL 1, HEAL <= 2, MOVE for plains, <= 2000 energy, MOVE parts in front), replaced when its ticksToLive < arrival (`(route rooms + 1) x 50 + 3 x parts`); one `controllerAttacker` (MOVE x N + CLAIM x N, N from the biggest base) spawned when the next attack (upgrade block end, seen or `lastAttack + 1000`) is within arrival + 50 ticks. Both travel room by room on the squad route (`route.ts`). Roles (`src/roles/siegeGuard.ts`, `src/roles/controllerAttacker.ts`, never throttled): the guard hits hostile creeps (workers, carriers, claimers and armed first; heals itself when not hitting), steps on hostile construction sites not under a rampart (spawn, tower first), else parks at range 3 of the controller; the attacker attacks once when the block is over and no safe mode, notes the attack, suicides the next tick (same tick could cancel the intent).
- Ends (`runSieges`, every military tick): controller without owner -> `taken` (log entry, `military:siege:taken`, the room released from its owner for remote mining and the expansion, `releaseRooms`); safe mode, a hostile spawn or a tower with >= 10 energy, another owner, 6 guards lost before their time or SIEGE_MAX_TICKS x 1.5 -> `abandoned` (log entry: `recentlyFailed` leaves the room FAILURE_MEMORY 20000 ticks, then it is an ordinary candidate again). Out of sight the siege goes on (the guard gives the vision back).
- Visible in the map / panel (`siege` row: room, owner, RCL, attacks, ticks to downgrade), the report (`siege W8N3 (enemy) RCL 1, 4 attacks`) and live-inspect (sieges table). Counters: `military:siege:start|attack|lost|taken|abandoned`.

## Safe mode timing and baits (2026-11-15, our design)

Problem (shard0 attack history, tick 79056568; room-history replays): Blindfold safe-moded W25N7 31 ticks after our boosted quad came in (79041260, 4 charges left, W29N4 and W28N3 hold 5), doorknobnob W26N7 the very tick our remote defenders crossed it (79041028). Each charge cost a whole mission. Worse, `updateAutoTargets` re-added any target with a mission after `targetStillHeld` had released it for its safe mode: the W25N7 quad poked the exit tile ~310 ticks under safe mode until `watchDrain` ended it as `abandoned`, the room then left alone FAILURE_MEMORY ticks. The intel never kept `safeModeCooldown`, so the ~30000 ticks after a safe mode where none can be activated were unused.

- Fix (`safeMode.ts` `refillEngaged`): the refill of engaged targets keeps only those `targetStillHeld` keeps; a mission whose target is seen in safe mode ends as `safeMode` (log result, not a failure: no FAILURE_MEMORY), before a stalled drain.
- Intel: `HostileRoom.safeModeCooldown` (tick) from `controller.safeModeCooldown`.
- Phases (`safeMode.ts` `safeModePhase`, pure, offline `node tests/safeMode/run.mjs`, in CI): `active` (safe mode on), `window` (no charge left, unknown charges on old records, or a cooldown of at least SAFE_WINDOW_MIN 5000 ticks: forming, trip, fight), `closing` (cooldown ends sooner: wait), `armed` (a charge can be used). Player candidates in `active` or `closing` are skipped with the tick they come back (`Memory.military.skipped`).
- Baits (`bait.ts`, role `roles/bait.ts`): a player target worth a squad (reason, feasible, ROI >= 1) whose safe mode is `armed` gets a 260-energy ATTACK x2 MOVE x2 bait instead of a squad, one at a time from the closest base with a spawn (priority 3 weight 1 like a squad), that hits the closest hostile structure (spawns, towers first). Seen in safe mode (by the bait itself every tick, or the intel): burnt (`military:bait:safeMode`, failures reset), the room is skipped until the safe mode ends, then a squad target in its window. One creep per bait: gone without a safe mode, or none within BAIT_TIMEOUT 1500 ticks, is a failure (`military:bait:failed`, `Memory.military.baits[room].failures`); after MAX_BAIT_FAILURES 2 the room gets squads directly. A bait that never spawned, a room no longer a bait candidate or one a squad attacks (manual attack included) ends without failure (`military:bait:dropped`). Bases: RCL `minRcl`+ (default 4) with a spawn.
- Fix found in review (mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl4 --enemy W8N3:3`, 5000 ticks): the refill of engaged targets must test the safe mode only, not `targetStillHeld` (which also drops a room without spawn): with it, the quad that razed W8N3's spawn at ~3360 was recycled as `retargeted` at 3400 with the tower still standing (no success, no loot, no siege).
- Unmeasured: the mockup enemy bot never activates a safe mode (`scripts/mockup/enemy-bot.js`), so the mockup only shows no regression; the bait is judged on shard0 by `military:bait:*` and the next mission results against Blindfold.

## Nukes (`military/nukeTarget.ts` pure, `military/nukeStrike.ts`; our design, 2026-11-15)

- Fill (`dispatcher/destinations.ts` NukerDestination, Hivemind's priority 1: only when nothing else waits): energy while the room's energy level is `high` or above (`core/resources.ts`: 50k without power spawn, 300k in a funnel room), ghodium only above what the labs' targets need (`labs/chain.ts` `spareOf`, `labs/reactions.ts` `spareGhodium`: a GH stock spares the G it was made from). The nuker's missing G is the last reaction target (kind `stock`: made from what the rooms hold, never bought), so the boosts come first. `construction/settings.ts` builds the nuker by default since this change.
- `Memory.military.nuke`: `false` neither fills nor fires (no G target either); unset (default) fills and fires only when told: `"W8N3"` (best impact from what is seen of the room: in sight, else the intel layout of any age) or `"W8N3:25,25"`, one strike, cleared once launched; `true` also fires automatically on the current targets (`currentTargets()`: a strategic reason or set by hand), players only (a stronghold's loot would burn), one nuke in flight per room, a layout seen within 5000 ticks. The closest loaded nuker within 10 rooms fires; `Memory.military.nukes[room]` keeps the flight, a notify and `nuke:launched` report it.
- Intel: `HostileRoom.layout` lists a player room's structures worth 8000+ (`STRUCTURE_WORTH`: spawns, towers, storage, terminal, labs, nuker, power spawn, factory, observer) with the hits of the rampart over each.
- Impact (`bestImpact`): every tile within 2 of a valued structure, the one destroying the most `STRUCTURE_WORTH` (the table our own defense protects by); a structure falls when the damage exceeds its rampart + its hits, earlier nukes of ours on the room eat into the ramparts first.
- ROI (`roi.ts` `nukeCost`, `nukeWorthIt`): 300k energy + 5000 G at its market price in energy; an automatic strike needs value / cost >= `MIN_ROI` (spawn + tower + storage without strong ramparts: 380k against ~305-350k; two spawns alone fall short).
- Synchronisation: a mission on a room with our nuke in flight waits until 1500 ticks before the impact (`missionWaits`, `nuke:missionHold`), and our creeps keep out of the target room until it lands (defense.md evacuation and border guard: every creep in the room dies at impact). The impact also ends a safe mode (but resets the cooldown: a safe mode charge left can be used again at once).
- Mockup (`--preset rcl8 --nuker`: a loaded nuker on the plan's tile; `--nuke-flight N`: flights of N ticks for the engine and the bot): W7N3 `--preset rcl8 --nuker --enemy W8N3:6:0:1000000 --nuke-flight 3000` (enemy spawn and 2 towers under 3M ramparts, a ring of 1M) with `attack: "W8N3"`, 6000 ticks: without the nuke (`nuke: false`) the duo then the quad never broke in (spawn and towers standing at 6000, 9 creeps lost); with `nuke: "W8N3"` (launched at 920 once the room was seen, best impact 33,24: spawn + 2 towers, 310k) the base fell at the impact (3920), 6 lost, among them a drainer on the exit tile killed by our own nuke: the squads are driven by the Military module after the CreepRunner, whose evacuation it overrode. With `nuke: "W8N3:33,24"` (launched at tick 20, the room never seen) the quad waited until 1520 (impact 3020 - 1500, `nuke:missionHold` 1497), its squads were left to the evacuation for 500 ticks (`nuke:squadsOut` 501), no creep lost, spawn and towers destroyed at 3020. Refill: after the launch the empty nuker took ~150k from the storage within 1500 ticks (energy level high: 50k in that room) and the squads the rest, the storage reached 0 by 4000 (5.7k at 6000 vs 20.4k without the strike).
