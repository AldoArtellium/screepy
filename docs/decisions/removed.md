# Removed features (2026-10-27)

Over-engineering review: features off by default, measured neutral, or overlapping CPU controls. The text below is what CLAUDE.md said about them, verbatim.

- Pixels and CPU unlocks (`src/modules/Pixels.ts`): off by default, untestable in the mockup and the private server (screeps 4.3.0 has no pixels); `decisionBucket()` reads the real bucket again.
- Spawn energy order and exit directions (`src/spawning/spawnPlacement.ts`): measured neutral; the engine's default order applies.
- Opt-in raids (`Memory.military.raid`, `loot.ts` `startRaids`, `roi.ts` `isRaidable` / `raidCost`, intel `towerEnergy` / `lootBarrier`): never enabled; looting after a successful mission is kept.
- Oversized harvesters under CPU pressure (`harvesterSizeFactor`): never on in the mockup, -4% points when forced.
- Reservations judged per CPU under pressure (`reservationPays`, `reservationValue`, `reserverCost` CPU pricing): only checked offline.
- Remote CPU budget raise rules: 75% spare share, 90% fit, 98% fit after 1000 ticks of full bucket (`BUDGET_LOW`, `BUDGET_FIT`, `BUDGET_FIT_FULL`, `BUDGET_FULL_TICKS`, `bucketFullSince`), replaced by one 95% share with the bucket as hysteresis.
- Military ROI: squad CPU priced in energy under pressure (`squadCpu`, `neutralEnergyPerCpu`) and the spawn time cost (`spawnTimeCost`, `squadMemberParts`, `spawnBusyShare`): the cost is the spawn energy and boosts with replacements again; the strategic reason stays the gate.

## Pixels and CPU unlocks (`src/modules/Pixels.ts`, not in Hivemind)

- Off by default: `Memory.pixels = true` turns everything on, `{ generate, sell, buyUnlocks, unlock, keepPixels: 0, unlockStock: 1, maxCpuRatio: 0.8 }` single settings (a setting not listed keeps its default). `maxCpuRatio` is a Memory setting: `Memory.pixels = { maxCpuRatio: 0.7 }` (default 0.8; it was 0.6, and shard0 at 15 of 20 CPU with a full bucket never made a pixel).
- Generation (every tick): `Game.cpu.generatePixel()` when the bucket is full, `Memory.stats.cpuAvg` below `maxCpuRatio` x the limit, the remote CPU budget uncapped (`Memory.remoteMining.maxRooms` undefined) and no owned room with a hostile not yet expected dead. Energy first: while the budget caps the remotes, a full bucket is its signal to add a remote room, not spare CPU. Absent on the private server and the mockup (screeps 4.3.0): nothing happens there, so it is only checked offline (`node tests/pixels/run.mjs`, in CI).
- Refill: after a pixel `Memory.pixelState.refilling` makes `decisionBucket()` (`utils/throttle.ts`) return 10000 until the bucket is full again, the CPU average reaches 80% of the limit or the remote budget gets capped. A capped budget is the only one that adds rooms on a full bucket (`bucket > 9000`), so it always reads the real bucket; an uncapped one can only lower (CPU above 95% still applies, a refill bucket below 5000 is hidden, as intended), and the budget changes at most every 1000 ticks, so the flag is cleared long before a capped budget could raise. Every bucket decision reads it (creep and module throttling, remote CPU budget, upgrader count, visuals redraw, planner, reclaim); the panel, stats and reports show the real bucket. Without it a pixel stopped the "others" roles below 2000 and throttled them up to 8000, with the default modules, for the ~1000 ticks a 20-CPU account takes to refill.
- Market (every 100 ticks, account resources: `Game.market.deal` without a room): use a CPU unlock when full CPU is not unlocked or ends within an hour (never with a subscription: `unlocked` without `unlockedTime`; not again within 10 minutes, an unlock can take 5 minutes to apply); buy 1 from the cheapest sell order while the stock is below `unlockStock`, at most 105% of the history average, keeping the MarketManager's 10000 credit reserve; sell the pixels above `keepPixels` to the best buy order from 95% of the history average. Nothing without price history.
- Counters `pixels:generated`, `pixels:sold`, `cpuUnlock:bought`, `cpuUnlock:used`; the report gets a `Pixels` line (stocks, full CPU hours left, credits) while enabled.
- Not measured on shard0 yet: at a 20-CPU limit the bot uses ~15-19 official CPU from RCL 4 with remotes; with the 80% gate and the budget gate, pixels come once every possible remote room is mined and the CPU average stays under 16.


## From "Economy core"

The order is cached per room and refreshed when the spawn+extension count changes or after ~1000 ticks +-10%; it falls back to the engine order if a structure is missing. Points within noise; total move intents +1% / +5% / +7%; `deliver:spawn` and `spawn:starved` unchanged. Kept as neutral, not a measured gain: re-measure on a quiet host with a per-role move counter.

## From "Economy core"

- Remote CPU budget (`RemoteMining.ts` `updateBudget`): an empire-wide cap on remote rooms from the tick CPU averaged over ~400 ticks (official CPU includes intents): above 95% of the limit or bucket < 5000 the worst scored remote room goes, below 75% with bucket > 9000 one comes back, and also below 95% once the (decision) bucket has stayed >= 9900 for 1000 ticks (`bucketFullSince`; shard0 W27N5 stayed capped at 7 rooms at 15/20 CPU with a full bucket); a room only comes back when it fits: average + one room's CPU <= 90% of the limit (`BUDGET_FIT`; one room = the `cpu:remoteHarvester|remoteHauler|reserver|remoteBuilder` counters over the mined rooms, averaged like the CPU, `roomCpu`, at least 1.2), and not within 5000 ticks of a drop (`budgetDropped`, `BUDGET_RAISE_HOLD`; drops are never held back). At most one change per 1000 ticks (`Memory.remoteMining.maxRooms`, counter `remote:budgetChange`; `maxRooms === undefined` = uncapped). The average takes every tick's CPU (heap sum, folded in at each selection with a weight of 1/400 per tick, `cpuAverage` / `budgetRooms` in `remoteIncome.ts`, checked by `tests/remoteIncome/run.mjs`), the tick of a global reset left out: one sample per selection (every 20 ticks) stayed in phase with the periodic modules (shard0: 19.3 for a real 16.5-17.5, 23 cap changes; mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl4 --cpu-limit 13`, 6000 ticks: 14.85 for a real 10.1, capped at 2 rooms; now 9.77 for 9.74, uncapped, 7 sources, remote harvest 147k -> 153k, storage 89k -> 96k, points 244k -> 240k; W1N3: 11.5 -> 9.9 for 10.2-10.3, never capped, 225k -> 227k). Mockup, `--cpu-limit 13` (~20 official), 20000 ticks: W9N5 85k -> 110k (RCL 2 at 12.7/13 CPU with 34 creeps: capped to 3 remotes, RCL 3 sooner, then uncapped), W1N3 183k -> 192k, W7N3 381k -> 355k (single runs). 85% / 70% thresholds oscillated and cut W7N3 by 27%. Since 2026-10-25 (shard0 at 18.5/20 CPU capped at 7 rooms with a full bucket, ~1.5 CPU per tick lost: the 90% fit with a 1.2 floor never let a room in, and its bucket dipped to 8964-9867 in every window so it never counted as full): one room's CPU includes its moves (`intents:move:<role>` x 0.2; shard0 0.86 per room, 0.48 code and 0.38 moves; the old total in `Memory.remoteMining.roleCpu` lacks the `moves` flag, so the first sample after the deploy restarts `roomCpu`), floor 0.8 (`BUDGET_ROOM_CPU_MIN`); the bucket counts as full from 9000 (`BUDGET_FULL_BUCKET`); after 1000 ticks of full bucket a room is added while average + room <= 98% (`BUDGET_FIT_FULL`); above 95% a room is only dropped below 8000 bucket (`BUDGET_DROP_BUCKET`, a margin under 9000 so one spike does not drop the room just added), above 100% or below 5000 bucket always. ## From "Economy core"

- Oversized harvesters (`harvesterSizeFactor`, `utils/throttle.ts`; Hivemind: from RCL 6, x1.5-2, local harvesters only): with a storage and `Memory.stats.cpuAvg` above 75% of the limit, local and established remote harvesters get twice the WORK (one harvest intent drains twice as much). Forced on (mockup W7N3, 20000 ticks, ticks 10k-20k): 27.6 -> 22.1 intents/tick (-20%, ~1.1 official CPU: harvest 7.3 -> 4.7, and fewer remote trips while the single spawn is busier), points -4% (3 runs vs 2: 390k vs 407k; less remote energy mined). Hence only under CPU pressure, where the remote budget would otherwise drop a whole remote room (~1.2 CPU). Never on in the default mockup (limit 100). Looked at and left: `reserveController` never ran at the cap (the reserver spawn rule already stops before it; a lower hold threshold only saves the one-off climb), remote builders only travelling when `needsRepairs` (no change: unbuilt road tiles keep it true), small transfers/withdraws (< 0.3 intents/tick), towers (only against hostiles).

## From "Economy core"

- Reservers (`remoteSpawnRole.ts` `reserverOption`, `RESERVE_HOLD`): a reserver beyond the first on its way only while the reservation left by those, decay included, over the new creep's life stays under 1000 (Hivemind: one per free tile around the controller; with 1-CLAIM bodies (energy capacity 650-1299) one reserver only offsets the decay and the 5000 cap never stopped them: shard0 W28N5 had 4 reservers from the RCL 3 W27N5, ~4.3 energy/tick). Mockup 12000 ticks, 1 run: W7N3 64 -> 50 reservers spawned, points 202.0k -> 201.4k, remote harvest 272k -> 275k; W1N3 68.0k -> 66.1k (1 / 5 reservers, RCL 3 late); W9N5 never reaches 650 capacity (no reserver either way: its 29.5k -> 10.8k is noise). Under CPU pressure (`remoteIncome.ts` `cpuPressure`: remote budget capped, or `Memory.stats.cpuAvg` above 85% of the limit) a room is only reserved when the reservation pays per official CPU (`reservationValue`, `remoteSpawnRole.ts` `reservationPays`; not in Hivemind, which reserves every room it can): the energy it adds (each mined source at 10 instead of 5 per tick, minus the bigger harvester and haulers and the reservers' energy) over the CPU it adds (reservers: code 0.06 + a reserve or move intent per tick over their life, one reserver holding `claimParts x (600 - distance)` ticks; haulers' extra trips: code + a move per tile + withdraw / transfer) against a neutral source's energy per CPU at that distance, which the budget would mine instead. Rooms mined for one source never pay (1-CLAIM, 50 tiles: 3.8 vs 5.3 energy/CPU; 2-CLAIM at 1300: 7.6 vs 8.4), two-source rooms do (5.7 vs 5.3, 11.2 vs 8.4); `node tests/remoteIncome/run.mjs` prints the cases. An invader's reservation is still attacked (the harvesters wait while it is above 500; another player's makes the room contested, see below, so it is dropped), so an invader core that reserves a room left neutral is still cleared. Haulers and harvesters of a room left neutral are sized for 1500 once our reservation has run out (live reservation when visible, else `lastClaim`). In the selection, `reserverCost` under pressure adds the reserver's CPU priced at that neutral energy per CPU, so a one-source room scores about its neutral income (50 tiles: reserved 6.3, priced 3.9, neutral 3.35) without a change in `RemoteMining.ts`. Reservers no longer call `reserveController` at the 5000 cap (an intent, 0.2 official CPU, for nothing). Remote haulers (checked, unchanged): they are already the biggest the energy capacity allows (`haulerSize`: equal haulers of at most capacity / 75 CARRY on roads, 17 at 1300, 24 at 1800, 33 max) and leave once they hold 90% or after a withdraw of min(1000, free capacity), so fewer trips would need more capacity, not another sizing. Mockup (6000 ticks, single runs, host loaded ~20 on 8 cores: wall-clock CPU, both bundles soon at the budget cap with 0-1 remote rooms, so mostly noise): W7N3 `--preset rcl5 --cpu-limit 13` 642.8k -> 652.2k points, 19 reservers both (W6N2, the fixture's only one-source room, was never left to choose); `--preset rcl5 --claim W8N3:3 --cpu-limit 15` reservers 20 -> 15, reserve intents 1.74 -> 1.30/tick, reserver CPU 0.77 -> 0.55/tick, points 627.8k -> 641.6k, storage 58.6k -> 73.6k. No cap (`--preset rcl5`, 4000 ticks): 609.2k -> 608.1k, remote harvest 178.9k -> 176.8k, 22 / 23 reservers, intents 19.2 / 19.1 per tick. To confirm on shard0 (W27N4 / W27N6 from W27N5).

## From "Military (`src/military/`, our own design, milestone 7 steps 1-2)"

- Raids (`loot.ts` `startRaids`, opt-in `Memory.military.raid = true`, every 100 ticks; Hivemind 3.0.0's `gatherer` without the walls it could not open): the intel keeps a room without owner, core or tower when it holds at least 10000 energy-equivalent of loot (an abandoned base), and records the hostile towers' energy (`towerEnergy`) and the strongest rampart over loot (`lootBarrier`); the containers next to a source in a room we mine are ours, never loot (the first run emptied W8N3's remote containers as the harvesters refilled them, so the mission never ended). A room is raided (`roi.ts` `isRaidable`) with intel younger than 5000 ticks, no core, no safe mode, towers under 10 energy, no armed creep seen and ramparts over the loot of at most 200k hits, from the closest RCL 4+ home with a storage within 5 rooms (one raid per home), when the loot pays for the looters and a dismantler (`raidCost`). The mission keeps the ramparts over the loot (`ramparts`, `covered`): they go to the home's dismantler (`remote/dismantle.ts` `dismantleTargets`), looters only spawn for open loot and wait while the rest is being opened. A tower seen with energy or an armed creep ends the raid (`defended`), the room is then left alone for 20000 ticks (`Memory.military.log`, squad `raid`). Counters `loot:raid:{started,done,abandoned}`. Mockup `--preset rcl4`, abandoned base in W8N3 (storage 40k energy + 10k U under a 100k rampart, empty tower; `--structures` with `owner`), 5000 ticks: raid from tick 700, the dismantler opened the rampart, 7 looters + 1 dismantler (~10k energy) brought 38962 energy + 2750 U home, 14k left at the end; with 1000 energy in the tower: no raid, nothing spawned. No module error.
