# Decisions: economy

Design notes and measurements moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-27 (verbatim). Features removed the same day are in `removed.md`.

## Economy core

Convention everywhere (`src/core/options.ts` `getBestOption`): **higher priority wins**, then higher weight; negative priority is never chosen; ties go to the first option collected.

- **Roles** (`src/roles/`, registered in `roles/registry.ts`): `harvester` (bound to a source, stationary on its planned harvest position = source container once a transporter exists, delivers like a transporter before), `transporter` (90%/10% hysteresis, orders revalidated every tick), `builder` (works from 90% full, builds/repairs, upgrades when nothing to do, never harvests), `upgrader`, `brawler` (RCL < 4 defense), `guardian` (RCL 4+, holds the rampart closest to the hostiles). `remoteHarvester`, `remoteHauler`, `reserver` (`src/roles/remote*.ts`, `reserver.ts`; flee hostiles that can fight, `roles/flee.ts`). `claimer`, `pioneer` (expansion, `roles/claimer.ts`, `roles/pioneer.ts`). The scout registers its handler in its module.
- **Spawning** (`src/modules/SpawnManager.ts`, `src/spawning/spawnRoles.ts`): each spawn role returns options (priority, weight); if the best option can't be paid yet the room waits (no cheaper fallback). Bodies from `src/core/bodyBuilder.ts` (exact port of Hivemind's BodyBuilder, `MoveMode`). Modules ask for creeps with `spawnManager.requestSpawn({ id, roomName, body, priority, memory })` on the same scale (harvesters 4-6, builders 3-4, remote/claimer 3, scout 1).
- **Dispatcher** (`src/dispatcher/`): `sourceDispatcher` / `destinationDispatcher` with providers (container, drop, grave, storage, overfullExtension / spawn, bay, tower, controller container, workerCreep, storage, drop at the base center). A creep's order lives in `creep.memory.order`; "reservation" = priority penalty per creep holding the same order, indexed per tick in `src/core/roomState.ts` (`creepsWithOrder`, `setOrder`, `clearOrder`). Transporter CPU profile (2026-10-26, mockup `--preset rcl6`, 3000 ticks, temporary `Game.cpu.getUsed()` counters, shares of the transporter's code CPU): cartographer `moveTo` ~34% (its cost matrix rebuilt per path search and per near-goal step ~12%: a loop over every structure for obstacles and roads; per-tick path cache bookkeeping ~13%; the native search ~3%), `getTask` ~30% (~0.33 calls per transporter-tick: a new order every ~4 ticks, ~1.2 calls per room and tick over 4 contexts, so a per-room per-tick cache of the providers' output would almost never hit: not done), `unblockBay` ~8%, executes without the move ~7% (intents included), validation ~3%. Changes: `unblockBay` first checks the plan's `bay_center` tile (O(1); was the bay list for every creep every tick: 0.052 -> 0.012 per creep-tick), providers sorted once per dispatcher (`highestPriority()` is a constant), and `TrafficManager` hands cartographer a per-room per-tick obstacle matrix (`structureMatrix`, `avoidObstacleStructures: false`; cartographer still loops the structures for roads, whose cost depends on the body: callback 0.090 -> 0.077). Same paths: the profiled before and after bundles, side by side on `--preset rcl6` 3000 ticks, end on the same points (1935706). A/B side by side, single runs on a loaded host, transporter code CPU per creep (CPU minus 0.2 x actions): W7N3 8000 ticks 0.432 -> 0.386 (-11%), W1N3 0.357 -> 0.339 (-5%), W9N5 0.473 -> 0.428 (-9%), `--preset rcl6` 4000 ticks 1.137 -> 1.097 (-4%, points 1952384 -> 1952712, deliveries unchanged); points W7N3 131.9k -> 129.5k, W1N3 34.3k -> 40.7k, W9N5 6.1k -> 4.9k (noise), no module error. Left: the rest of cartographer's cost (road loop, path cache serializers) needs a patched cartographer.
- **Room state** (`src/core/roomState.ts`): per-tick caches (creeps by role and home room, by duo / squad (`duoCreeps`, `squadCreeps`), structures by type), source/controller containers, storage location (plan center before the storage), path length storage location -> controller container (`controllerRefillPathLength`, shared by spawning and dispatcher, not cached while the container is missing), effective energy, bays.
- **Planner** (`src/planner/`): Hivemind steps (center, harvest positions, upgrader spot + controller container, exit corridors, core, harvest bays, extension bays, ramparts on the min cut around the protected structures (`minCut.ts`), safe area (`safe` tiles; nothing built outside), towers placed greedily by rampart coverage plus 0.5 x their cover of the core (spawns, storage) and built closest to the center first (not in Hivemind: far chokepoints pulled every tower 10-20 tiles away from the base on W29N7), roads to the ramparts, on-ramps, `link.controller` next to the upgrader spot (older plans get it added on load, no replan); after the bays the helper parking spot, 10 labs on Hivemind's stamp (2 `lab.source` within range 2 of every other lab; fewer kept when no stamp fits, Hivemind fails the variation), power spawn, nuker and observer), packed in the `plans` segment store (`roomPlanner.planMemory(room)`: `k` variation, `s` score, `done`). Variations (`listVariations`): a center ("weighted", then the tile farthest from walls in each cell of a 3x3 grid; a room with a spawn keeps its center next to it) x every combination of sources with or without a spawn in their bay; scored by `RoomPlanScorer.ts` (structures, upkeep, tower cover, ramparts near exits, travel distances). The first variation is stored at once (the economy needs a plan from tick 1), the others are tried over the next runs (CPU budget 50% of the limit) and the best replaces it while nothing but the first spawn is built; a room without spawn waits for the final plan before building. Bump `PLAN_VERSION` (`RoomPlan.ts`) to replan every room. `PLAN_VERSION` 4 added the labs and high level structures: a version 3 plan (W29N7 live) is not replanned but extended in place (`extendRoomPlan`: parking, labs, power spawn, nuker and observer on the free tiles of its safe area, nothing built moves; the run.mjs check rebuilds each fixture's v3 plan, extends it and checks nothing moved). Offline check: `node tests/planner/run.mjs [ROOM...]` (all variations, scores, defense geometry: ramparts, safe tiles, tower ranges to the center in build order, tower cover of the core and of the ramparts; ASCII plan of the best in `test-results/plan-<room>.txt`, ~22 ms per variation in node). Fixtures in `tests/planner/data/` (`W29N7` = the live room, `W29N7-live` with its existing spawn).
- **Construction** (`src/construction/RoomBuilder.ts`): Hivemind order — RCL 1 spawns; RCL 2+ extensions bay by bay, source and controller containers, swamp roads to sources/controller; RCL 3 harvester-bay extensions, towers, ramparts once a storage exists (main ones, then on-ramps; not during a safe mode > 2000 ticks; see Defense); RCL 4 storage, then every planned road once the storage is built; RCL 5+ links (controller, then source links farthest from the controller, then storage: with 2 sources RCL 5 = controller + 1 source, RCL 6 = both sources, RCL 7 = storage); RCL 6 terminal, extractor and mineral container; once every main rampart holds 500k x maxWallHealth[rcl] / 20M hits (Hivemind checkWallIntegrity: 50k at RCL 6), labs (`lab.reaction` first while 3 are allowed); nuker, power spawn, observer and factory only when enabled (`Memory.construction = { nuker, powerSpawn, observer, factory }`, all off by default, `construction/settings.ts`, Hivemind `settings.default.ts` constructNukers...; nothing fills, fires or uses them yet, the plan keeps their tiles) (Hivemind buildEndgameStructures, rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-endgame.md`). Mockup W7N3 `--preset rcl8 --labs`, 8000 ticks: the baseline built the power spawn, observer and factory and had a nuker site by tick 6000-8000; now none, labs unchanged, storage 89k -> 244k.
- **Idle once built** (Hivemind room-manager `builtAllStructures`): a room whose full pass leaves no site, places none and puts nothing off (a creep on an obstacle tile, the global site cap) is skipped (heap `completeRooms`) until its RCL, plan (`v`/`k`/`s`), structures (count and position sum: decay, destruction), home roads of the remote paths, a long safe mode, the construction settings or the wall-integrity gate (only re-checked when the endgame waited for it) change, else a full rescan every `CREEP_LIFE_TIME` +-10%. Counters `roomBuilder:scan` / `roomBuilder:idle`. Mockup: RCL 8 `--labs` 8000 ticks RoomBuilder 0.457 -> 0.053 CPU/tick; from scratch 20000 ticks the room is rarely complete (W7N3 0.47-0.51 -> 0.42, W1N3 half the runs idle).
- **Stale sites** (`construction/siteCleanup.ts`, The International `international/constructionSites.ts`): every 100 ticks the first tick each of our sites was seen is kept in `Memory.siteFirstSeen` (dropped when gone); a site older than 20000 + 5 x progress ticks is removed (counter `sites:removedStale`), freeing the room cap (5) and the global one (100) from sites in remotes given up, failed expansions or lost rooms. Never fires within 20000 mockup ticks; checked with a 300-tick limit (51 removals, no error).
- **Room cleanup** (`src/construction/RoomCleanup.ts`, module `RoomCleanup` every 100 ticks; Hivemind `room/room-manager.ts` cleanRoom :242-330 and dismantleUnwantedDefenses :504-525): in owned rooms without hostile creeps (`destroy()` returns ERR_BUSY then), other players' construction sites are removed and their structures destroyed at once, storage and terminal included (the ruin keeps their content and is looted by the dispatcher's grave source). `room.storage` also returns another player's storage: waiting to loot it first left the room at RCL 1 with progress 0 (mockup W7N3 with a 20k leftover storage; baseline d2de81b: still RCL 1 at tick 8000). Once the plan is final: unplanned walls (not respawn / novice area walls: no `hits`, `utils/structures.ts` `isIndestructible`; shard0 W24N8's 37 border walls have `hitsMax` 300M, a real-time `decayTime` and no `ticksToLive`, which the old test relied on, so the cleanup "destroyed" them every 100 ticks while the engine dropped the intent; offline check `node tests/roomCleanup/run.mjs`, in CI), and our extensions, labs, links and containers on a tile planned for another structure (or an extension, lab or link on a planned road). Never our spawns, towers, storage, terminal or ramparts. Counters `cleanup:<type>`. Not ported: Hivemind's rebuild order for a room at RCL 3+ that lost its spawn (tower ramparts and towers before the spawn).
- **Links** (`src/modules/Links.ts`, `roomState.ts` `linkNetwork`, rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-links.md`): planned `link.controller` next to the upgrader spot (older plans get it added on load, no replan), `link.storage` in the core, `link.source` in each harvester bay. Network per tick: source links "in" (want 0), controller link "out" (wants 800), storage link neutral. Every 3 ticks one transfer per room (biggest surplus or fullest neutral -> biggest shortfall or emptiest neutral, at least 200; counter `link:transfer`). Harvesters fill their source link before the container (in a bay through `Bay.refillFrom`); upgraders withdraw from the controller link first (body "easy access" with a link too); dispatcher `link` source (neutral links while the network holds more than it wants) and destination (neutral links while the controller link lacks energy), priority 5, -1 beyond 10 tiles. Transporters: Hivemind base amount minus 1 + linked sources once the controller link exists; not in Hivemind (its `estimateNeededCarryParts` TODO), the haul estimate leaves out the share of the linked sources' output the links carry (all of it with a storage link, else what the upgraders' WORK draws, farthest source first). The controller container is still refilled by transporters (as in Hivemind). Measured (mockup, baseline state at tick 22000 / 32000 forced to RCL 5 with `--set-rcl 5`, 12000 ticks, one run each on an overloaded machine): W7N3 points 1.067M -> 1.064M (-0.3%), storage 136k -> 150k, 91k energy through the links, code CPU 15.5 -> 15.1, intents 24.7 -> 24.6/tick; W1N3 (storage ~20k, upgraders on the 10k threshold) 648k -> 612k, storage 19k -> 24k: the two links cost 10k energy, which delayed the upgraders by ~5000 ticks. RCL 6 (3 links): no errors.
- **No hub hauler** (decided, not built): Hivemind serves the storage link with transporters through the dispatcher (priority 5 when within 10 tiles). With Hivemind's build order the storage link only exists from RCL 7 in a 2-source room (RCL 6 with 1 source), so a stationary creep at the storage (Overmind `commandCenter`, The International `hubHauler`) would have nothing to move at RCL 5-6. Revisit with the terminal (RCL 6+), where it would also balance storage / terminal.

- **Remote mining** (`src/remote/`, rules in `docs/hivemind/hivemind-remote.md`): every 20 ticks each owned room with a plan keeps at most 5 remote rooms (4 from RCL 4) within 3 rooms, its sources ranked by net energy per tick per spawn part, see deviations (`Memory.remoteMining`; paths in segment 5, cached 5000 ticks +-10% per source, failures 500; one copy per source, packed by `utils/pathCodec.ts`: start position + 1 direction digit per step, `node tests/pathCodec/run.mjs` checks it, decoded paths cached in heap; `remotePath(source)`). Spawn role `remote` (one option per tick, closest source first): hauler (once the container exists and the source has a remote harvester, CARRY = 2 x travel x energy/tick / 50), harvester (priority 3, the first one included, see deviations; places, builds and repairs the container), reserver (from 650 capacity, one per free tile around the controller). Haulers deliver at home through the destination dispatcher. A remote with a dangerous hostile (or on the way) gets no spawn until the hostile is expected dead.
- **Storage era (milestone 3)**: remote builders (`roles/remoteBuilder.ts`, Hivemind `builder.mines`; only with a storage and 5000 effective energy, priority 3, weight 0, one per home at a time, WORK sized on the real need, see below) step along the remote path. "Needing repairs" (`remote/repairs.ts` `repairsDue`, offline check `node tests/remoteRepairs/run.mjs`, in CI; Hivemind: container below half or a road missing or below 2/3): container below half, a road missing or below a third of its hits, or 50000 hits missing on the path; a builder also only picks such a source, else it waits at the storage. shard0 2026-10-25: 6 remote builders (~1.5 CPU per tick with their moves, ~5 spawn energy per tick) walked 85% of their ticks to repair ~2 energy per tick while the 7 remote rooms missed ~2200 energy of hits in all (W28N6: 92). The builders repair/build the road tiles i-2..i+2 around them and the container, and refill at the home storage. In the home room, `RoomBuilder` places the roads of the remote paths at RCL 4 with a storage. Builder demand (`remote/repairs.ts` `builderWork` / `pathNeed`, `RemoteMining.ts` `repairNeed`, `remoteSpawnRole.ts` `builderOption`; Hivemind: builders of equal size until their WORK covers 0.7 + 0.03 per path tile of every source): shard0 2026-10-26 (W27N5, 20 CPU, CPU at 19.2) kept 4.7 remote builders of 33 parts (1950 energy) on average, ~1.4 CPU per tick (code ~0.13 + 0.84 move intents per creep) and ~4k spawn energy per 1000 ticks for ~3.9 energy per tick of work (`work:remoteRepair` ~1.8k + `work:remoteBuild` ~2.0k per 1000 ticks) while no remote road was below a third and the containers stood at ~249k/250k. Now a home has at most one remote builder alive (spawning included), spawned only while a mined source `repairsDue`; its WORK = the energy the due sources' paths miss (hits missing on container and roads / 100, plus construction sites' progress left, a missing road 300 (1500 on swamp), a missing container 5000) over what one WORK spends in a life (`energyPerWorkLife`: a 125-energy load per trip, trips of 2 x travel + 125 ticks over 1500 - travel: 538 at 100 tiles, 207 at 300), at least 2 WORK (4 while something is to build), capped by the body the home can spawn (spawn reason `<n> WORK for <k> source(s)`). Rooms out of sight count the need last seen there (heap, per source and room, 5000 ticks), else nothing. The selection's cost (`maintenanceWork`, `remoteIncome.ts` `sourceNet` / `sourceParts` / `sourceCreeps`) is now the steady upkeep: `upkeepWork` = tiles x 0.003 energy per tick (decay plus wear, from the shard0 repair rate) x 1500 / `energyPerWorkLife`, a fraction of the home's builder (100 tiles at 100: 0.84 WORK, was 4).
